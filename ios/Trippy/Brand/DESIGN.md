# trippy design language

## Core direction
Warm, clean, rounded, playful, and personal. The supplied brand sheet is the source of truth: a soft ivory canvas, the supplied coral/peach/yellow balloon-pin logo and dark forest-green wordmark, generous typography, and destination photography with warm light. The app should feel like a personal starting point for creating a trip, not a filing cabinet of saved plans.

## Palette
- Forest #102A22 — logo, primary text, main actions
- Coral #FF685B — selected states and action accents
- Amber #FFBE55 — warmth and discovery
- Peach #FFCDBD — soft highlights and supporting surfaces
- Ivory #F8F3EC — main background
- Sage #477461 — secondary accents and status

## Type
Plus Jakarta Sans. Use bold rounded hierarchy for destination names and stage titles, regular-weight copy for supporting information, and keep helper text brief.

## Components and motion
Rounded photo cards, soft ivory input surfaces, small segmented chips, and restrained coral borders for selected choices. The Stage 1 carousel is the main event: a small balloon mark, one clear plus action, a centered photo card, narrow side previews, smooth swipe and subtle depth. Keep one primary action visible at a time. Stage 2 reveals the essential trip choices first and folds detailed preferences into one expandable section. Stage 3 uses a destination-photo hero, an overlapping information panel, collapsible day cards and compact bottom navigation for itinerary, map, places and costs.

## Image direction
Use generated editorial destination art for trip covers and atmospheric banners. Keep the images warm and believable, with careful mobile crops and no baked-in text. For real places, show verified venue images and map data from trusted sources rather than invented documentary-looking imagery.
