# trippy brand assets

`Trippy-Logo-Variations-Brand-Sheet.png` is the user-provided source. The app uses clean transparent crops from it:

- `BrandWordmark.imageset`: coral/amber balloon mark with the forest-green trippy wordmark for light appearance.
- `BrandWordmarkDark.imageset`: the same supplied lockup with a warm ivory wordmark for dark appearance; this avoids artifacts in the sheet's pre-rendered white variant.
- `BrandMark.imageset`: standalone balloon mark used in the new-trip card and launch screen.
- `AppIcon.appiconset`: the supplied balloon mark on a full-bleed ivory background.

The exports preserve the supplied silhouette and color direction. The dark wordmark is recolored from the clean primary crop; the rest are direct crops. Six warm, AI-generated destination covers are in `../Assets.xcassets/Trip*.imageset/`, with their original contact sheet here. Asset prompts and usage guidance are in `docs/ASSET_GENERATION_PROMPTS.md`.

The app uses Plus Jakarta Sans in `../Resources/`, distributed under its included SIL Open Font License.
